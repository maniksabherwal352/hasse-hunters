📜 HOW TO PLAY

❤️ Lives

You enter every room with 3 lives. A mathematically invalid submission costs 1 life — but you always get a proof of what went wrong. Lose all 3 and the room resets with fresh lives.

✦ XP & 🗝 Keys

First-attempt solve: +100 XP. After one hint: +75. After two hints: +50. Streaks add +10. Each conquered room grants a key that unlocks the next chamber.

◈ Relation forge

Rooms 1–2 are a directed playground: click one node then another to toggle an arrow, click a node twice for its loop ↺, click any arrow to remove it. Drag nodes anywhere. There are no multiple-choice questions — you manipulate the structure until the live lamps turn green.

⬣ Hasse rules

Draw only cover relations: no loops, no transitive edges. Greater elements go higher. Click one node then another to draw an edge; click an edge to remove it. Drag nodes to arrange them (they snap to their rank). ↺ undo / ↻ redo are always available. The forge never lists the answer — it only tells you what kind of mismatch remains.

💎 Diagram play

Treasure hunts, peaks, crowns and bounds are all played on the diagram itself: tap glowing nodes to collect them. Fresh structures are generated every replay and difficulty adapts to you.

👑 Final boss

Type your own set and relation pairs. The engine analyzes it live. Then prove mastery on your own diagram: treasures, peaks, least/greatest crowns, LUB/GLB.

Guessing is not enough — every verdict is computed by the math engine and explained step by step.
